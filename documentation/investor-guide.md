# Investor guide

**Sandbox notice:** this version of Mynx is a demonstration. Balances, payments and investments are simulated and have
no monetary value. A banner at the top of every page says so.

## 1. Open an account

1. Choose **Open an account**. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 12 characters. If a friend sent you a
   referral link, their code is filled in for you.
2. Confirm your email from the message we send.
3. Sign in. We recommend turning on **two-factor authentication** under **Security**: you will then enter a code from
   your authenticator app when you sign in.

## 2. Verify your identity

Before you can deposit, invest or withdraw, complete **Verification**:

- personal details and address
- your income band, source of funds and investment experience
- a short risk questionnaire, which sets your risk profile
- photos of your ID document (front and back) and a selfie, as JPEG, PNG or PDF files

We review your application and email you when it is approved, or tell you exactly what to change.

## 3. Add money

1. **Wallet → Deposit:** enter an amount. You go to the payment provider's page to pay. In the sandbox, choose "Simulate
   successful payment".
2. The money appears in your **available** balance once the provider confirms it. If you close the page early, the
   deposit status page checks with the provider for you.

## 4. Invest

1. **Marketplace:** compare products by return, term, minimum and risk level (1 is lowest, 7 highest). Each product
   page lists its fees, risks and documents. Read them.
2. **Can I invest?** shows whether you can, and why not if you can't.
3. Enter an amount and tick that you understand the risks. If a product is riskier than your profile, you are told and
   must confirm separately.
4. Review the summary and **Place order**. The amount is **reserved** in your wallet until our team confirms the
   investment, and you can cancel while it is pending.

**Returns:** "target" returns are not guaranteed. On your Portfolio, fixed-rate investments show an **estimated** value
(interest earned so far, less fees). Others show **at cost** until they mature. **Realized** means money actually paid
to you.

**At maturity:** the proceeds go to your wallet. To reinvest automatically in the same product, choose **Reinvest at
maturity** on the holding. If the product's terms change before then, nothing is reinvested and the money stays in your
wallet.

## 5. Withdraw

**Wallet → Withdraw:** choose mobile money or a bank account in **your own name**, enter the amount and confirm. The
money leaves your available balance straight away and is sent once our team approves the request. You can cancel
while it is under review. If a payout fails, our team retries it or returns the money to your wallet.

## 6. Other currencies

If more than one currency is enabled, **Wallet → Convert** moves money between your own currency wallets. You see the
rate, the fee and the exact amount you will receive before confirming. The quote is fixed for 60 seconds.

## 7. Statements and documents

- **Documents:** create an account statement, portfolio statement, transaction report or yearly tax statement, as PDF
  or CSV. Every document you create is kept, so you can download it again.
- **Receipts and confirmations:** on **Transactions** (deposit and withdrawal receipts) and **Portfolio** (investment
  confirmations).
- **Tax statements** list your income and, where rules are set up, an estimate. They are not tax advice, and nothing is
  deducted from your payments.

## 8. Notifications, referrals and help

- **Notifications:** the bell shows updates. Under **Preferences**, choose email, SMS or in-app per topic. Security
  emails are always sent.
- **Refer a friend:** share your link. You are rewarded only when your friend verifies their identity and invests,
  under the current campaign's terms.
- **Help:** answers are in the **Help centre**. Otherwise open a request under **Support**, with attachments if useful,
  and we reply there and by email. Choose "Make a complaint" for complaints; we track each one until it is resolved.

## Staying safe

- We never ask for your password or authentication codes by email, SMS or phone.
- If you get a "new sign-in" email you don't recognise, change your password and review **Security → Active sessions**.
- Withdrawals only go to accounts in your own name.
